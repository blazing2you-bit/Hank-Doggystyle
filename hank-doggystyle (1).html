⚠️ To activate Hank on Telegram you need to deploy the index.js webhook server to Railway (free). Once deployed, set your webhook URL and Hank will respond to all messages and commands from your phone.
Deploy steps
1. Go to railway.app → New Project
2. Upload the hank-telegram folder (index.js + package.json)
3. Railway auto-deploys — copy your URL (e.g. hank-bot.railway.app)
4. Open this link once in your browser to activate:
https://api.telegram.org/botYOUR_TOKEN/setWebhook?url=https://YOUR_RAILWAY_URL/webhook
5. Open Telegram → find your bot → type /start 🐾
